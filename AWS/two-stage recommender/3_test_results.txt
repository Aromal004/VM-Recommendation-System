   - Zero variance across 10 random seeds

   - Weight stability: std < 0.0002
   - Highly reproducible results

5. SCALABILITY
   - Full pool (8000 instances): 12.34 seconds
   - Sub-linear scaling with instance count
   - Production-ready latency

6. COMPONENT CONTRIBUTIONS (Ablation)
   - Fit score: Critical (47x improvement in right-sizing)
   - Hard filtering: 4x latency reduction
   - BO optimization: Maintains quality with lower latency
   - Diversification: Prevents family concentration

   - AMD: Good balance of performance and cost
   - Intel: Highest compute but lowest efficiency

The proposed two-stage pipeline achieves superior workload fit (81x better
